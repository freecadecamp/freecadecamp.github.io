Welcome to Varam Sustainable Solutions

We are manufacturers of biomass smokeless cooking stoves, pellet batch stoves, pellet continuous feeding stoves, pellet gasifiers, biogas plants, charcoal BBQ grills and accessories, biomass pellets and briquettes, country firewood logs, and lump charcoal for grill cooking and fire pits.

Our Focus

Pioneering Research & Eco-Friendly Performance

At Varam Sustainable Solutions, we have spent the last 7 years conducting extensive research and innovation to develop high-quality, efficient smokeless stoves designed for homes, hotels, and industries.

Our advanced smokeless stoves burn wood, pellets, briquettes, and charcoal while maintaining clean, eco-friendly, and cost-effective performance.

7 Years of Innovation

Dedicated R&D to optimize thermal efficiency and achieve complete combustion.

Eco-Friendly & Clean

Reduces indoor air pollution by burning wood, pellets, briquettes, and charcoal with virtually zero smoke.
